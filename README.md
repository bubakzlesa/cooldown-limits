# Cooldown Limits

Cooldown Limits is a simple personal habit app built around one idea:

**After you do something, start a cooldown and clearly see when it is available again.**

👉 **[Open the app](https://bubakzlesa.github.io/cooldown-limits/)**

It can be used both for reducing bad habits and for keeping good habits on schedule.

## Why I made it

Some habits are easier to manage when the rule is concrete.

Instead of thinking:

> “I should probably eat less pizza.”

you can define a simple rule:

> “Pizza at this place: once every 14 days.”

When you do it, tap the tile. The app starts the cooldown and shows when the activity is available again.

For example:

- 🍕 Pizza — once every 14 days
- 🍔 Fast food — once every 7 days
- 🎮 Buy a game — once every 30 days

The same idea also works well for **good habits**.

For example:

- 🏋️ Workout — once every 2 days
- 🧹 Clean apartment — once every 7 days
- 📚 Review a subject — once every 5 days

After a workout, tap the tile. It becomes unavailable for two days. When it becomes ready again, the dashboard immediately shows that it is time to work out.

So the app is not only about avoiding things. It can also answer a very simple question:

**What should I do today?**

## How it works

Each habit or activity is represented by a tile.

A tile can be:

- **Ready** — you can or should do it now.
- **Cooling down** — the app shows how much time remains.

Tap a ready tile when you perform the activity. Its cooldown starts immediately.

You can create your own tiles and choose the cooldown for each one.

## Install on Android

The app can be hosted with **GitHub Pages** and installed as a **Progressive Web App (PWA)**.

Open the GitHub Pages version of the app in Chrome on Android and choose:

**Install app**

It will then appear on the phone like a normal app and can be opened from the home screen or app launcher.

The app is designed to work offline after it has been loaded and cached by the browser.

## Privacy

The app does not require an account or a backend server.

Your limits and history are stored locally in your browser on your device.

Use the built-in export function if you want to keep a backup of your data.
