# Cooldown Limits

Cooldown Limits is a small personal habit app for spacing out limits and keeping routines on schedule.

👉 **[Open the app](https://bubakzlesa.github.io/cooldown-limits/)**

## How it works

Each activity is represented by one of two tile types:

- **Limit** — for something you want to keep spaced out, such as pizza or buying a game. Tap it when used and it stays locked until its cooldown ends.
- **Routine** — for something you want to repeat, such as a workout or cleaning. The tile shows when it is due, but you can log it at any time. Logging early restarts the interval from that moment.

Examples:

- 🍕 Pizza — limit to once every 14 days
- 🎮 Buy a game — limit to once every 30 days
- 🏋️ Resistance workout — repeat every 2 days
- 🧹 Clean apartment — repeat every 7 days

Tap a tile to record an available limit or log a routine. Use the menu on a tile to see its history, undo the latest entry, or edit its settings.

## Install on Android

Open the app in Chrome on Android and choose **Install app**. It will appear on the home screen and app launcher like a regular app.

After its first successful load, the app works offline. When a new version is published, it is downloaded through the app's service worker during a later online visit.

## Privacy and backups

The app has no account or backend. Tiles and history stay in browser storage on your device and survive app updates.

Use **Export backup** in Administration to download your data as JSON. Use **Import backup** to restore it later or move it to another device.
